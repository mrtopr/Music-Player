Mehfil — mehfil (महफ़िल), an evening gathering for music and poetry.

Mehfil began as a personal build: a player that gets out of the way and lets the record speak. It grew into an open-source project — developed in public, shaped by the people who use it.

There is no ad business here. No analytics quietly shipping your habits somewhere. Just a music player, its source code, and a community that believes listening should be simple and free.
